# IEEE METSB — Vice Chairman Plan

## Vice Chairman Plan — 2026/2027

### Based on and parallel to the Chairman's Plan (Ali Abdel Nasser)

---

## 00 | Core Idea of the Vice Chair Role

If the Chairman's equation is:

> Develop the Member → Build the Leader → Unite the Board → Expand the Branch

then the complementary equation of the Vice Chair is:

> **Track the Plan → Catch the Gaps → Hold the Line → Be Ready to Lead**

The Chairman sets the direction, represents the branch externally, and makes the final decision. As Vice Chair, I am responsible for ensuring that this direction is translated into day-to-day execution within the Board, and for serving as the second line of defense if there is any deviation from the plan or an unexpected absence.

**The fundamental difference between me and the Chairman:**

| Chairman | Vice Chair |                                     |
| --- | --- |
| Strategic vision and direction             | Daily follow-up and execution                  |
| External representation (SAC, other branches) | Internal consistency across Cycles          |
| Final decision                    | Escalation and early warning             |
| Building the Framework                | Implementing the Framework in practice       |
| Owner of the full Context           | Guardian of Context implementation across every Cycle |

This plan **does not repeat** what the Chairman stated in his plan. Instead, it details the execution gap that my role fills in each of the sections he defined.

---

## 01 | Executive Summary of My Role

The Chairman identified the main weaknesses in his summary as: uneven Cycle strength, difficulties in PR, FR, and Media, and weak Marketing. My role as Vice Chair is to be the **execution mechanism** that monitors these gaps weekly, not just seasonally, and raises them to the Chairman before they become strategic problems.

In other words: the Chairman sees the big picture of each Cycle or Event. I am expected to see the detailed picture every week.

**My three core roles throughout the season:**

1. **Deputy** — I execute the Chairman's decisions and represent him in any meeting or situation he is absent from.
2. **Auditor الداخلي** — I periodically review every Cycle and Head against the established plan, rather than waiting until a problem occurs.
3. **Successor المُجهّز** — As I am a natural candidate for the Chairman position in the future, I need to document and learn from every decision the Chairman makes, not merely execute it.

## 02 | Current Situation Assessment — My Perspective

The Chairman stated that the branch is "operational, but regular communication with every Cycle may create Silos." This is exactly where I am expected to focus.

### My diagnostic responsibility at the beginning of the season:

- Conduct a **Baseline Audit** for every Cycle during the first two weeks of the season: identify the active members, what is working, and what is stalled.
- Document the current strengths and weaknesses of every Cycle in a single table (Google Sheet or Notion) and review it weekly with the Chairman.
- Identify any "lone hero" (a Head handling everything alone without delegation) — this is an early warning sign of the Silos the Chairman warned about.

### The difference between me and the Chairman here:

He identifies the strategic problem (a weak Pipeline). I identify its **daily symptoms**: which Head is not responding on time, which HR team is not sending reports, and which Cycle has gone three weeks without a Meeting.

## 03 | Strategic Vision and Objectives — My Role in Each Pillar

The Chairman defined four pillars: Leadership Pipeline, Independence & Member Growth, Network & Branch Presence, and One Direction One Board. I am not adding a fifth pillar; I am the **person responsible for measuring the implementation of all four pillars**:

| Pillar | My Role |                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | --- |
| Leadership Pipeline          | I actively track which members are developing toward Head/Vice roles and maintain a live Talent Map (not only at the end of the season, as the Chairman himself proposed in Proposal 8). |
| Independence & Member Growth | I monitor whether members rely on the Chairman/Vice for every small step or have actually become independent — a direct indicator of this pillar's success.                         |
| Network & Branch Presence    | I act as the Chairman's deputy in any external representation when he is busy — meaning I must stay informed about all communication with SAC and other branches continuously, not only when needed.     |
| One Direction, One Board     | This is the pillar closest to the core of my role — I am the executive responsible for ensuring that every Cycle understands the same Context.                                                      |

## 04 | Preparation Phase — My Role

The Chairman begins by sending the Returning Members Form and then selecting a Head/Vice for each Position.

**My role during this phase:**

- I provide a **second approval** for every Head/Vice selection before it is finalized — not to compete with the Chairman's decision, but to provide a documented Second Opinion, especially against the criteria he himself defined (Knowledge → Leadership → Communication → Teamwork → Commitment).
- I prepare and manage the Interviews schedule (times, rooms or links, and coordination with candidates), while the Chairman and D&R focus on the content and questions themselves.
- I document every Interview in a unified table (strengths/weaknesses of each candidate) — this will serve as an important reference in case of any dispute or later review.
- I attend the Season Alignment Meeting as a Facilitator — the Chairman leads the vision, while I ensure that every Head leaves the meeting with a clear, documented understanding of their role.

## 05 | Board Development — My Role

The Chairman divided training into Core Training + Customized Training.

**My specific role here:**

- **I am the executive responsible for scheduling the Core Training** (Communication, Leadership, Teamwork, Time Management) — I book the venue/link, coordinate with speakers or trainers, and track attendance.
- After the Core Training, I manage the **Individual Needs Assessment** for each Head personally — a short (30-minute) session to directly understand each person's needs, then submit a summary to the Chairman before Customized Training is decided.
- I follow up on the implementation of the recommendations resulting from the Assessment — I should not simply conduct the assessment and leave it; I must track whether the customized Session was actually delivered.

**الفرق:** الـ Chairman بيقرر *إيه* التدريب required استراتيجيًا. أنا بتابع *هل happened actually* وبجمع الـ Feedback afterward.

## 06 | Recruitment I — My Role

Here my role is closer to that of an **Operations Manager** for the entire process, while the Chairman and D&R define the strategy and criteria.

**Specifically:**

- I manage the actual Timeline: when the Form opens and closes, when the Webinar takes place, when the Interviews happen, and when the Orientation takes place — and ensure that every step follows the timing defined in the Roadmap.
- I review the First Filtration with HR (weak or duplicate applications) before they reach the Interviews stage, so the Heads' time is not wasted.
- I serve as the primary point of contact with applicants immediately after submission (acknowledging receipt and reminding them of Interview dates) — the Chairman should not be the person responding to every individual message.
- I operationalize the Chairman's principle of "Convert Interest While It's Hot": I ensure that the gap between application and Interview does not exceed X days (to be determined with the Chairman; my proposal is no more than 5–7 days).

---

## 07 | Orientation & Selection — My Role

- I handle the logistics of the entire Orientation day (venue, Ice-breaking, and division into Cycle Groups) — the Chairman opens and welcomes participants, but the actual organization is my responsibility.
- I review the distribution of new members across the Cycles with HR before announcing it, to ensure balance (for example, avoiding placing all the strongest members in one Cycle).
- I personally send and follow up on the Accepted/Not Accepted messages in coordination with HR, and ensure that every accepted member has clear Orientation details.

---

## 08 | First Application & Member Training — My Role

The Chairman defined the methodology: We train the Board → The Board trains the Members → Members apply through real work.

**My role in activating this methodology:**

- I follow up with each Head during the Intensive Training Week: are they actually training their members on the Workflow, Tasks, and expectations, or merely distributing tasks without explanation?
- I attend a sample of these training sessions (not all of them, but a random sample) to verify the quality of the training itself, not merely that it took place.
- I coordinate with the Event Management Head on the first pilot Event, and ensure that there is a clear gap between training and execution (as requested by the Chairman) before launching a real event.

---

## 09 | Continuous Member Development — My Role

Here the Chairman defined the cycle: Training → Event → Evaluation → Development Need → Targeted Session → Next Event → Reevaluation.

**I am responsible for operating this cycle in practice:**

- After every Event, I collect each Head's evaluation of their members during the same week (not two or three weeks later) — delay here destroys the value of the cycle.
- I maintain a Central Tracker for every member across all Cycles — what they needed, whether it was provided, and what the result was in the next Event.
- I provide the Chairman with a **monthly summary** (not a detailed report on every member) of the overall development trend so he can make strategic decisions based on it.

---

## 10 | HR System — My Role

- I am the **direct escalation point for the HR Head** for any recurring problem or Pattern (absence, declining performance) before it reaches the Chairman formally — meaning I am the first escalation filter.
- I review the monthly HR Report before it reaches the Chairman and ensure that it is based on documented data rather than impressions.
- I follow up on the implementation of the Interventions proposed by HR (Session, one-on-one conversation) — whether they actually happened within the specified timeframe.

---

## 11 & 12 | R&D Strategic Unit & Event Planning — My Role

The Chairman described R&D as a parallel unit that plans for the future while the branch operates in the present, and which is monitored by him or a dedicated Head.

**My role here:**

- If the Chairman directly oversees R&D, I serve as the **operational** follow-up point between R&D and the Cycles — R&D prepares the Plan, while I ensure that it reaches the Cycle Head sufficiently ahead of the event (the Timeline outlined in the plan: Research → Design → Review → Alignment → Execution Follow-up).
- Specifically during the Alignment stage, I review the Draft before it reaches the Chairman for final approval — an additional correction point before execution.
- After every event, I follow up with the Secretary to ensure that the R&D & Event Report was actually written and not forgotten amid the event workload.

## 13 | Board Communication System — The Core of My Role

The Chairman explicitly stated that the goal of the Main Board Group is to break the Silos, and that the Heads are responsible for communicating with the rest of their Cycle. This system **must be under my direct operational supervision**.

**Specifically:**

- I manage and monitor the Main Board Group daily — not merely as a member, but by tracking whether all Updates actually return to the Board as outlined in the Communication Path (Chair/Officers → Whole Board Context → Heads understand roles → Heads delegate → Cycles execute → Updates return to Board).
- I conduct a **brief weekly Check-in** with each Head (even if it is just a short text message) — not a full meeting, simply asking: "What is working, and what is stuck?" This is a practical, lightweight way to prevent Silos from accumulating until they affect the entire communication loop.
- If I notice that a particular Cycle is becoming isolated from the others (information is not reaching it, or it is not sharing updates), I raise it to the Chairman immediately before it grows.

## 14 | Cycles Strategy — My Role

The Chairman divided the Cycles into FR, PR, Logistics, and Marketing/Media, and identified the needs of each one (Redesign or capability development).

**My role here:**

- I pay particular attention to the Cycles the Chairman identified as "needing capability development" (PR, FR, Media according to his plan) — not to manage them instead of the Head, but to monitor their progress or stagnation more closely than the other stable Cycles.
- I coordinate directly between FR and the Treasurer (the plan itself warned about role conflicts between them in Proposal 7) — I serve as the clarification point if there is any confusion about responsibilities.

## 15 | Cross-Cycle Task Exposure — My Role

- I follow up with the Event Management Head on the list of members available for Cross-Cycle Exposure and ensure that the distribution is fair and does not repeatedly involve only the same members.
- I use this list as a direct input for updating my Talent Map (Proposal 8 in the Chairman's plan) — every member who demonstrates readiness here is added to the "Ready Soon" list.

## 16 | Officer Simulation & Season Events — My Role

The Chairman established three main events with different levels of escalation (Officers Lead → Officer Simulation × 2).

**My role in the first event (Officers Lead):**

- I am one of the Officers who directly leads the execution — this is the event where I actually "work," not merely monitor.

**My role in the second and third events (Officer Simulation):**

- I am the **direct supervisor** (Supervision) of the Officer Simulation team in coordination with the other Officers, and I provide direct Feedback after every decision they make during the simulation.
- I document the performance of every member of the Simulation Team — this is the most important data source for the Talent Map before the end of the season.

## 17 | Recruitment II — My Role

The same logic as my role in Recruitment I, but with stricter criteria (Quality over Quantity, as defined by the Chairman):

- I personally review every application before the Interviews at this stage (the number is smaller, so this is feasible) to ensure that the "quality over quantity" principle is actually applied rather than remaining just a slogan.
- I manage the Integration Activities for the new cohort so that they integrate quickly with the existing branch (Ice-breaking + Cross-Cycle Sessions mentioned in the Chairman's plan).

## 18 | Board Responsibilities & Officers — My Position in the Structure

The official description of my role in the Chairman's plan:

> "قيادة مساعدة للـ Chairman، تعده في Follow-up وExecution، وتحويل الresponsibleيات عند absence"

**In detail, this means in practice:**

| Responsibility | Details |                                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | --- |
| Follow-up                 | I review the progress of every Cycle weekly against the seasonal plan (Roadmap, Section 22).                                                                                |
| Execution                  | I execute any decision made by the Chairman at the level of daily operations.                                                                                         |
| Deputy Role During Absence       | If the Chairman is unavailable (travel, emergency), I lead the Board meeting and represent the branch in any urgent communication with SAC or the Section, while informing him of every decision made immediately upon his return. |
| Maintaining Alignment | I serve as the first line of defense against Silos between the Cycles (detailed in Section 13 above).                                                                          |

**Limits of My Authority (Important to Clarify from the Start):**

- **I do not make decisions** on major strategic decisions (changing the branch direction, major financial decisions, changing a Head) without consulting the Chairman — except in the case of his complete absence and genuine necessity.
- **I make decisions** on daily operational details (scheduling, coordination, resolving minor conflicts between Cycles) without needing prior approval every time.

## 21 | Organizational Learning & Handover — My Specific Role

This section is especially important to me because it is precisely where I prepare myself for the Chairman position in the future (if that is the goal).

- I personally document **every major decision** the Chairman makes throughout the season, including the reasoning behind it (not just the outcome) — this is a personal archive for me, separate from the official Handover report.
- I attend every Handover meeting, if available (even from previous seasons), as an observer and learner.
- At the end of the season, I prepare a **separate Vice Chair report** (not a replacement for the Chairman's report) documenting what I observed from my operational perspective, the gaps I identified between the plan and actual execution, and my recommendations for who should take the role after me (Chairman or Vice).

## 23 | Suggestions for Developing My Role (Parallel to the Chairman's Suggestions)

1. **A written agreement with the Chairman on authority limits** — Before the season begins, the decisions I can make independently and those requiring consultation should be defined in writing (even if on a single page), especially during his absence.
2. **A fixed weekly Check-in schedule** with every Head — not randomly, but on a fixed day and time each week, even if it is only 10 minutes per person.
3. **صلاحية دخول (Access)** لevery أدوات Follow-up (نماذج HR، تقارير الـ Cycles، الـ Central Tracker) from day one — so my follow-up role is not blocked by missing information.
4. **A compact version of every Template** used by the Chairman or R&D (such as the Event Brief Template proposed in Proposal 5 of his plan) — I use it as a standardized reference during review and follow-up.
5. **A mid-season review of my own role** — Not only a review of the branch's performance; there should also be a session with the Chairman to review whether the division of roles between us is working properly and whether it needs adjustment.
